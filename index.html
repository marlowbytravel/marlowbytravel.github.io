About Marlowby

One U.S. city a month, packed with the local goods, snacks, and neighborhood picks worth bringing home.

OUR APPROACH

Skip the Gift Shop.

Most travel boxes feel like a random pile of souvenirs. We built Marlowby to send you the stuff locals would actually buy.

Each month, we pack 5–7 full-size snacks, coffee beans, and everyday goods from real local shops in a different U.S. city plus a fold-out neighborhood map in case you visit one day.

12 Cities A new U.S. city every month
5–7 Items Full-size local goods in each box

Real Local Picks

Everything inside comes straight from independent shops in that month's featured city.

Stuff You'll Actually Use

Regional coffee beans, hot sauces, ceramics, and local snacks; not cheap items that end up forgotten

A Real Neighborhood Guide

Every box includes a custom city map with local food spots, hidden spots, and weekend itineraries.
